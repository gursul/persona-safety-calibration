AI usage declaration (draft; to be updated before submission)

We used Claude (Anthropic; Claude Code, Sonnet model family) throughout this project:

- Code: Claude wrote and debugged the scripts for data preparation, generation, judging,
  sampling, label correction and statistical analysis, and the repository documentation.
  The authors ran and checked the code and the numbers it produced.
- Annotation: Claude acted as an LLM annotator. It labeled 379 distinct model responses
  (compliance, refusal, deflection) following the written guide in docs/labeling_guide.md,
  without access to the judge's labels at the time of labeling. One author labeled
  an independent subset by hand (first pass 59 responses; further subsets pending) and
  discussed borderline cases with Claude. The reliability of the labels is reported in the paper.
- Analysis design and explanation: Claude proposed statistical methods and helped explain
  them; the authors chose the research questions, hypotheses, label policy and conclusions.
- Writing: [to be completed once the paper is drafted: describe exactly which parts were
  drafted or edited with Claude.]

The subject model (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) and the automatic judge (Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct) are
objects of the study and are described in the Methods section, not AI assistance.

The authors take full responsibility for the content of the paper and the repository.
